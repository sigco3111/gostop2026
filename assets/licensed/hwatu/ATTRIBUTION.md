The master SVG is retained here. The classic card WebP files in
`public/assets/cards/classic/` are rasterized and cropped from this SVG.
The drawings and colors are not changed. Cards are reordered for the game's
Korean month/score classification, including paulownia in November and willow
in December. These derived card images are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
No endorsement by the artists is implied. This artwork license does not change
the license of unrelated game code or separately created artwork.
